### Key Requirements

1. **Only generate new records related to this task** - do not duplicate existing data.
2. Generate 2-4 records per involved service (including 1-2 task-critical signal records + 1-2 noise records).
3. IDs must start from the "Currently Available Starting IDs" and increment sequentially. No conflicts with existing IDs.
4. Cross-service references must be consistent (customer names in emails must match CRM, products in tickets must match inventory).
5. All dates must be strictly within the time window {time_window}. No date (timestamps, due_date, date fields, or dates mentioned in text content) may fall outside this range. Additionally, the bulk of this task's dated records (calendar events, emails, meeting notes, todo due_dates, transaction dates, ticket creation/update times, etc.) must cluster within roughly ±7 days of the scenario anchor date {scenario_anchor_date}. A small number of references may extend further only when the scenario explicitly demands it (e.g. a quarterly review naturally spans ~90 days). Do NOT default to placing everything near the end of the time window.
6. `signal_records` marks the task-critical signal record IDs (used by grader for scoring) - must be a subset of records.
7. All record fields must strictly follow the Schema definitions.
8. If existing entities (company names, person names, etc.) can be referenced, prefer referencing them for data coherence.
9. All text content must be in English.
10. Timestamps must respect the work schedule: emails, meetings, tickets etc. should occur during working hours unless the scenario explicitly requires otherwise (e.g. on-call alerts, system monitoring).
