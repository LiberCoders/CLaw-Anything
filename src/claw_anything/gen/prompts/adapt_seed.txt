## Your core instruction

Instantiate the **seed's challenge pattern** as a task that falls into the **target content direction**, adapted to the persona.

- The **cognitive mechanism** comes from the seed (its description / key_actions / safety_concerns define the puzzle shape).
- The **surface domain** comes from the target content direction (the agent-visible task should read as a `{target_content_direction}` task).
- The **interaction mode** stays fixed at `{seed_interaction_mode}` — a `patrol` seed stays a proactive-discovery task, an `overview` seed stays a daily overview, a `standard` seed stays an explicitly-requested reactive task. Do not change the trigger style.
- If the native bucket differs from the target direction, re-cast the mechanism into the target domain. Example: a "hidden-conflict" pattern with target `scheduling` becomes a task where the hidden conflict lives between calendar and chat-log evidence; with target `workflow_execution` it becomes a workflow where mid-step evidence contradicts the initial plan.
